Behind You — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

Behind You ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is created by Om Kadam. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding your data when using the Behind You iOS application.

1. Apple HealthKit Data (Crucial Notice)

Behind You accesses Apple HealthKit data through Apple's official HealthKit APIs with your explicit permission. Specifically, the App requests permission to read the following metrics:

  • Step Count (HKQuantityTypeIdentifierStepCount)
  • Walking and Running Distance (HKQuantityTypeIdentifierDistanceWalkingRunning)
  • Active Energy Burned (HKQuantityTypeIdentifierActiveEnergyBurned)

HealthKit Privacy Guarantee (Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.3 Compliance):

  • Your Apple HealthKit data is used solely to progress your in-game runner, advance game environments, calculate distance from the demon, and display your personal activity dashboard.
  • Behind You never writes or alters any data in Apple Health.
  • Behind You does not use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or use-based data mining.
  • Behind You never sells, rents, or discloses HealthKit data to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers under any circumstances.
  • Behind You does not store HealthKit data in iCloud. All HealthKit queries are processed strictly in memory and saved only to your local on-device database.

2. Motion and Pedometer Data

The App utilizes Apple CoreMotion pedometer data to detect live movement when you are actively in a challenge. This enables real-time animation of your runner on screen. Motion data is processed ephemeral in memory on your device and is never transmitted off your device.

3. Local Storage and SwiftData

Your challenge history, selected character, world preferences, and personal best records are stored locally on your device using Apple's native SwiftData and UserDefaults frameworks. This data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the application.

4. No Third-Party Tracking or Analytics

Behind You does not include any third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics frameworks (e.g., no Google Firebase, no Meta/Facebook SDKs, no AppsFlyer, etc.). We do not track your activity across other apps or websites.

5. Live Activities and Dynamic Island

When you start a walking challenge, Behind You may display a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island so you can track your real-time distance from the demon. Live Activity data is managed entirely on-device by iOS ActivityKit.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Since Behind You does not require you to create an account or provide personal credentials, all game data resides strictly on your iOS device. You can clear your challenge history within the App's settings or completely erase all stored data by uninstalling the application from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

Behind You does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The App does not collect personally identifiable information from any user.
