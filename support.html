Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do my steps move the character?
Behind You connects to Apple HealthKit. When you walk in real life, iOS records your step count and passes it to the game. Walking forward increases the distance between you and the chasing demon.

Q: Why are my steps not updating?
Make sure you have granted Health permissions to Behind You. You can check this in your iPhone Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Behind You, and ensure "Steps" is enabled.

Q: Does the app track my GPS location?
No. Behind You does not track or store your GPS location. Gameplay is driven solely by your step count and motion cadence.

Q: Can I use the app on iPad or Apple Watch?
Behind You is designed natively for iPhone with Dynamic Island and Live Activity support.
